3 Piece - Printed Lawn Suit

Rs. 5,990

Description

Unstitched 3 piece suit with a printed lawn shirt, a dyed cotton trouser and a printed chiffon dupatta. Soft, light fabric that is comfortable for everyday summer wear.

What's Included

  • Printed lawn shirt front: 1.25 m
  • Printed lawn shirt back: 1.25 m
  • Printed lawn sleeves: 0.65 m
  • Embroidered neckline patch: 1 piece
  • Printed chiffon dupatta: 2.5 m
  • Dyed cotton trouser: 2.5 m

Fabric & Design

The shirt is made from fine 100% cotton lawn with a soft hand-feel that breathes well in hot weather. The floral print is digitally printed so colours stay sharp and do not bleed after washing. The chiffon dupatta has a matching print with a light, flowing drape, and the cotton trouser is piece-dyed in a coordinating shade.

This design is part of our summer collection, inspired by traditional block-print motifs reworked in a modern colour palette. It can be stitched as a straight shirt with cigarette pants, an A-line kurta, or a classic shalwar kameez.

Care Instructions

  • Hand wash or gentle machine wash in cold water.
  • Wash dark and light colours separately.
  • Do not bleach.
  • Dry in shade to protect the print.
  • Iron on medium heat on the reverse side.
  • Dry clean the dupatta for best results.

Shipping & Returns

Orders are delivered within 3 to 5 working days across Pakistan. Free delivery is available on orders above Rs. 5,000. Unused items with tags attached can be exchanged within 14 days of delivery at any of our stores or through our online customer care.

Actual colours may vary slightly from the images because of screen settings and lighting during photography.

Size Guide

As this is an unstitched suit, the fabric quantity above is enough for sizes XS to XL. For larger sizes, please contact customer care before ordering so we can advise on extra fabric.
